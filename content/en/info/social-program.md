### Social program included with 4+ weeks courses

* 2 guided tours in Assisi
* 2 pronunciation classes
* history of music class
* guided tour at the St. Francis Basilica
* food and wine tasting

### Facilities at the Accadamia available to any student

* free wi fi and internet point
* music room with piano for pianists and singers
* video library with classics of cinema and documentaries
* library (Italian language textbooks and literary classics)

### Extra activities arranged upon request

* biking. Bicycle tours with a guide
* hiking. Nature walks with a guide
* riding. Horse back riding with an instructor
* two-days trips (Saturday and Sunday) to Italian cities such as Rome, Florence, Venice.
* guided excursions in the area surrounding Assisi.
* guided visits to museums, monuments and places of interest in and around Assisi.
* meetings with sculptors and painters in their working environments.
* lectures concerts, meetings with writers and poets.
* outings to well-known Umbrian towns such as Todi, Gubbio, Perugia, Deruta, Spoleto.
