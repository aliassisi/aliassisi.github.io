## Apartments

At walking distance from the Accademia, fully-furnished apartments with two sets of bed linens, bath
towels, self-catering with a fully equipped kitchen and wi-fi.

| Type of accommodation             | 2 Weeks       | 4 Weeks       | Additional days |
| --------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------- | --------------- |
| Single room in shared flat        | 280€          | 430€          | 25€             |
| Double room in shared flat        | 200€ / person | 320€ / person | 15€ / person    |
| One bedroom flat (2 persons max.) | 480€          | 650€          | 35€             |

#### The price includes

- Wi-fi access
- Two (2) sets of bed linens and bath towels
- Use of washing machines (there are also laundromats in Assisi)

#### The price does not include

- Utilities (water, electricity, gas), which will be calculated based on actual consumption.

#### The Following are not included but available at an additional fee:

- Cleaning services: 25-50 Euros charged for each cleaning
- Parking: parking spaces are available for about 40 Euros per month at the ParkSi lot in Piazza Matteotti
- With the Accademia students’ card, students have price reduction (6 to 18 € for a meal) at selected
  restaurants, pizzeria and trattoria.

#### Additional important points

- Please remember that smoking is not permitted in the apartment.
- You are not able to host friends or family in the apartment in either single or double rooms
- Please let us know in advance if you are not able to use stairs or if you have any other mobility
  limitations

#### Arrival and check-in

Please plan to arrive between 4 PM – 8 PM. We ask that you send an email to accommodation@aliassisi.it to
communicate your exact arrival time. Please let us know if you are not able to arrive between 4 PM – 8 PM
so that we can inform the apartment owner.

#### Departure and check-out

Please arrange to check-out before 10:00 AM.
