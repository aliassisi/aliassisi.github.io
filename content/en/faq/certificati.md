title: "Which certificates are issued by the school?"
draft: false
type: "courses"
---

At the end of the course the Accademia issues an attendance certificate.

According to the agreement signed with the Università per Stranieri di Siena in January 2008, we issue a
certificate with:

* the crediting of any credits obtained after having passed at Accademia Lingua Italiana Assisi the courses final
exams according to the quality criteria of the Siena University centre;
* the recognition of modules completed at ACCADEMIA LINGUA ITALIANA ASSISI.
