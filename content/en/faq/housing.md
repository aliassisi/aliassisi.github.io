Our accommodations are located only in the centre of the city. Max 5 minutes walking distance from the school.
The flats are furnished and the kitchens are supplied with all essential utilities (cutlery, plates, pans etc.).
Costs of heating and electricity not included and are to be paid according to consumption (average cost: winter
70/110 euro per month; summer 40/70 euro per month). The apartments are cleaned before your arrival. Bed-linens
are provided. There is the washing-machine. There is no telephone in the flats, but you can access the free wifi
access of the Accademia and use skype. Italy uses a 220 Volts/AC system. Plugs may differ from those in your home
country and it’s possible that you need an adapter. Available for the period of one day before the start of the
course until one day after the end of the course. Each extra day costs € 25.
