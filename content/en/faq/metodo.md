We use a communicativie and holistic method, our student-centrered approach makese students progress quickly and
with a clear understanding of the intermediate learning steps. We use Italiano in and Arte e metodo. In addition
to the coursebooks, we offer authentic material. Our courses are designed to give an introduction to Italian
culture through literary texts, newspapers, films, works of art, songs, culinary traditions, all designed to give
you a taste of Italian language and culture.

From the first day on, at all levels, only Italian will be spoken!
