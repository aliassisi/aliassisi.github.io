---
title: "What happens with a cancellation?"
draft: false
type: "courses"
---

If a cancellation is made 30 days before the beginning of the course, the school refunds the total amount of the
deposit (Euro 260).
If a cancellation is made up to 15 days before the beginning of the course, the school retains 50% of the deposit.
For cancellations on a later date, the school retains the whole deposit.
