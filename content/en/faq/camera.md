Flats are located only in the centre of the city. Max 5 minutes walking distance from the school. Flats are
reserved only for students of the Accademia. In each flat kitchen and bathroom are of common use. Double rooms
are available for two students who register toghether and ask to share a double room. The flats are entirely furnished and the kitchens are supplied with all essential utilities (cutlery, plates, pans etc.).

Costs of heating and electricity are not included and are to be paid according to consumption. (average costs:
winter 70/110 euro per month; summer 40/60 euro per month). The flats are cleaned before your arrival. During the
stay, you and your flatmates are responsible for the cleaning of your room and of the rooms used in common
(kitchen and bathroom). Bed-linen is provided. Each flat has a washing-machine. There is no telephone in the
flat, but you can access the free wifi at the Accademia and use skype. Italy uses a 220 Volts/AC system. Plugs
may differ from those in your home country and you may need an adapter. Flats are available for the period of one
day before the start of the course until the day after the end of the course. Each extra day costs € 25 for
single room and € 15 per person for a double room.
