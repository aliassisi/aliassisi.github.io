You’ll receive via mail a detailed map and the the owner’s contact information. For those who arrive at Assisi
train station we suggest to reach the city centre by bus (there are buses every 30 minutes, ticket price: 1,30
euro, 2 euro on board) or by taxi (about 2 km, 15/20 Euro).  Taxis are always available at the train station (or
you can call radio taxi Assisi +39 075 813100).
