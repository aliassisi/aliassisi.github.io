 * **Class size**: 1

Individual lessons provide students with tailored classes specifically addressing their individual needs. Advanced and
intermediate level students can choose the topics they would like to study and can tailor their lessons to focus on the
relevant vocabulary and content for their professional/academic fields. By taking individual lessons, beginner level
students can develop a strong foundation in the Italian language in a very short period of time.
