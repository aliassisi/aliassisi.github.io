 * **Class size**: 3

Lessons for three provide students with tailored classes specifically addressing their needs. Advanced and intermediate
level students can choose the topics they would like to study and can tailor their lessons to focus on the relevant
vocabulary and content for their professional/academic fields. By taking lessons for three, beginner level students can
develop a strong foundation in the Italian language in a very short period of time.
