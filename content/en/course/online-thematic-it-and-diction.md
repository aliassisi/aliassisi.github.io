 * **Morning Class Size**: 4 - 12 students
 * **DMorning Class Hours:**: 4 hours/morning with a 15 minute break
 * **Afternoon Class Size**: one-on-one lesson
 * **Afternoon Class Hours**: 2 hours
 * **Days per Week:**: 5 days (Monday through Friday)
 * **Levels**: Beginners, A2 through C2
 * **Credits for a 4 weeks course:**: 4 CFU (ECTS) / 3 Neumann University Italian credits (at appropriate level)
 * Pronunciation classes included

Six hours of lessons each day made up of four hours in the morning in a regular class with up to 12 students and two
intensive hours in the afternoon in a one-on-one lesson.

This course combines group work and individual work. The structure of the Italian and Diction course is well-suited for
all levels of students seeking maximum results in a short period of time. The afternoon one-on-one sessions are
dedicated to improving the student’s pronunciation and listening abilities and to helping the student master the unique
characteristics that make Italian “la bella lingua” including volume, intensity, rhythm, and pauses.
