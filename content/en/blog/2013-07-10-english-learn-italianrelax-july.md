NUN-Assisi Relais & Spa Museum ![](http://www.aliassisi.it/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/nun11-1024x76.jpg)








It is an exclusive 5 star Relais that came about from the careful restoration of the ancient monastery of Saint Catherine, which was originally constructed in 1275 for a community of Benedictine nuns. Its 18 suites are situated a short distance from the center of Saint Francis’ city in a quiet natural setting. The ancient place has preserved the details of the mediaeval monastery, designed along the lines of Benedictine rules calling for a “human city” for individuals. Each ample room is unique, with niches, frescoes, cross-vaulted ceilings and yet with all the modern comforts of technology which pleasantly surround the body and the mind with simple elegant formality in complete harmony with the spiritual nature of the ancient stones.







Lessons







Private course of Italian language (14 hours), after which is issued a certificate of attendance and evaluation. The Academy teachers have a university background and years of experience.










Books










Books and workbooks will be distributed at the beginning of the course. The Accademia is also open outside the class schedule: a library, video library, multimedia educational materials for individual use and the wi-fi are available.










Enrolment







**The program costs 1320 Euro each for accommodation in triple room, 735 euro each for double room and 550 euro for a single room**.




The program includes: - private language and culture course (60 minutes hours, 14 hours) - course books - certificate of attendance - certificate of evaluation - 3 meals with your tutor - 3 days in NUN-Assisi Relais & Spa Museum including 2 nights, breakfast buffet and free entrance in the Spa of the hotel to do thermal path - guided tour in Assisi - guided tour to the[ Eremo della Carceri ](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eremo_delle_Carceri)- free wi-fi













**Extra activities**







Assisi, Umbria area, weekend art










Assisi is in Umbria, 120 kilometers from Florence and 200 from Rome (easily reached by [train](http://www.trenitalia.it)). Umbria is famous not only for Assisi but for many other medieval towns: Spello, Gubbio, Spoleto, Perugia, Montefalco, Trevi, Bevagna Castiglione del Lago, Todi. In Accademia, you can book guides and transport (minibus or taxi), or weekends in art cities (tours + transportation + accommodation in Rome, Venice, Florence, Pisa, Padua).










**Shopping**: on request we organize transport for: 1. luxury outlet: a.    _The Mall_ with the following brands: Giorgio Armani, Fendi, Gucci, Hogan, La Perla, Tod's, Valentino, Alexander McQueen, Bottega Veneta, Burberry, Emanuel Ungaro, Ermenegildo Zegna, Salvatore Ferragamo, Sergio Rossi, Stella McCartney, Yohji Yamamoto, Yves Saint Laurent. b.    _Valdichiana outlet_: Baldinini, Benetton, Basile, Calvin Klein, Bialetti, Coveri, Merrel, Nike, Ginori, Replay, La Murrina, Massimo Rebecchi, Marina Militare, Marina Yachting







2. the most famous companies producing cashmere based in Umbria: Arnaldo Caprai, Maria di Ripabianca, Brunello Cucinelli.




3. the historical cellars of the Rosso di Montefalco and the museum of the legendary wine cellars of Lungarotti.
