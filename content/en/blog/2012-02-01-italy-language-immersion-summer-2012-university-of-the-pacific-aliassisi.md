## Dates


May 21-June 29, 2012

Spend 6 weeks this summer in the beautiful Umbrian hill town of Assisi, home of Saint Francis, and earn 8 extension units equivalent to first year college Italian.


## Features





	  * 140 hours of intensive small-group instruction with highly trained native speakers
	  * Shared apartment living with kitchen
	  * Group excursions in the region and to Rome and Florence



## Cultural Activities





	  * Concerts and lectures on music
	  * Guided tours of famous sites of Assisi
	  * Cooking class and Medieval dinner
	  * Sampling of typical products of Umbria
	  * Art lesson
	  * Italian Cinema

For more information, please [click here ](http://www.pacific.edu/Academics/Services-and-Resources/International-Programs-and-Services/Education-Abroad/Find-a-Program/Current-Faculty-Led-Programs/Italian-Summer-Language-Immersion-Summer-2011.html)![picture of students on Italy trip in 2009](http://www.pacific.edu/Images/ips/Italy%20group%20pix.jpg)
