# Courses available during the summer of 2020

We are currently working remotely and are conducting our coursework via
_virtual classrooms_ to ensure the safety of our students and teachers.
It is a temporary way to provide continuity in our work and to avoid any
disruptions for the many students who have already begun their courses
of study in Italy.

We hope that it will not be long until our lessons are once again offered
in teh bright classrooms of the Accademia, a meeting place for students
of all nationalities.
