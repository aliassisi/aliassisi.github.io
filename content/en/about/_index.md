The Accademia was founded in 1993 by Angelo Chiuchiù. He previously taught Italian
as a Second Language at the University for Foreigners in Perugia (Università per
Stranieri di Perugia) and worked for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Ministero
degli Affari Esteri - MAE) as a teacher’s trainer.

In the 1980s, he published one
of the most widely used coursebooks for learners of Italian as a second language
(In italiano). The project was promoted by the Presidency of the Council of
Ministers (Presidenza del Consiglio dei Ministri) and RAI television network
produced a video which was broadcasted internationally.

The Accademia is recognized
by the Italian Ministry of Education (MIUR) and has agreements with Universities,
Conservatories, and Fine Art Academies. We receive students from international
universities including: Neumann University (Aston, Pennsylvania), University of the
Pacific (Stockton, California), Elon University (Elon, North Carolina), Pontifical
North American College (Rome, Italy), and Pontifical Irish College (Rome, Italy).
Our courses attract an eclectic mix of learners including high school and
university students; musicians; professionals preparing to move to Italy; scholars
learning Italian to deepen their research in Italy; individuals with a passion for
the Italian language and culture; and C1-level students who are training to become
Teachers of Italian as a second language.

Each afternoon, students have access to
the music room and library and are tutored by our teachers and course directors.
Many of the students take advantage of these resources every day.

### Teachers

Our teachers have at least a university education and have been further trained at
the Accademia Lingua Italiana Assisi. They collaborate in the preparation of new
e-books, online activities, and self-study activities. We promote ask-based
activities in order to ensure that students develop their Italian speaking
abilities both inside and outside he walls of the classroom. Our teachers are
examiners for PLIDA, Roma Tre, and CILS. They have permanent contracts.

### Assisi

Assisi is the perfect town for students interested in an immersion experience as
they are living amidst the locals. In addition, Assisi is located in the region of
Umbria, known as the green heart of Italy. Students can easily reach cities ike
Rome and Florence in less than two hours by train. Assisi is a historic city that
is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage site. In addition to its architectural and
artistic delights, it is the city of St. Francis of Assisi. Umbria s known for many
other medieval towns including Spello, Gubbio, Spoleto, Perugia, Montefalco, Trevi,
Bevagna, Castiglione del Lago, and Todi.

### Location

The location of Accademia Lingua Italiana Assisi is situated in the historic heart 
of the town centre, at 10 via Tiberio d’Assisi, a short distance from Piazza del 
Comune and in the vicinity of the Basilica of St. Francis and the Church of Santa 
Chiara.

This 14th century  former residence was renovated in 2006 by the architect Michele 
Bilancia. With a great sense of respect for the history of the building,  architectural 
changes have been made in order to enhance the functional properties of the space.

The classrooms and offices are accessible from a medieval courtyard. All the classrooms 
have air conditioning. The second floor balcony boasts a panoramic view of the entire 
town and the Umbrian valley.
