### About

Angelo was an Italian professor at the University for Foreigners of Perugia
from 1975-1994. He taught classes and held seminars in second language methodology
in both in-service programs and refresher courses that were set up by the Ministry
of Education and Ministry of Foreign Affairs. He has delivered pedagogical lectures
on Italian as a second language all over the world including the USA, Canada,
Brazil, Peru, Mexico, Argentina, Uruguay, Venezuela, Hong Kong, Singapore, Germany,
Austria,, Greece, Hungary, Russia, Malta, Tunisia, Albania, Spain, Portugal,
France, Finland, Australia, Egypt, Croatia, Korea, Lebanon, Slovenia and Great
Britain. He has published articles on methodology and is the author of numerous
works including: In italiano, a multimedia course (text, audio, and handbooks in
English, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Greek, Russian, Romanian, Hungarian, Polish,
Korean, and Chinese); the new Italiano in; Viva l’italiano, a multimedia course for
children (four books and a video); Le preposizioni; I verbi italiani; L’italiano al
laboratorio linguistico; Et cetera; Quaderni d’italiano; and Ambienti. He is a
consultant for the video courses In italiano and Viva l’italiano produced by the
Italian Television (RAI) under the auspices of the Council of Ministers of Italy.
He is the author of Comunicare in italiano (Hoepli) and In italiano. Il corso 1, 2,
3 (Hoepli).
