### About

Gaia graduated with a degree in Applied Linguistics from the University of
Perugia.

She is responsible for research experimentation, and programming for the
courses offered at the Accademia. She is also the coordinator of the Italian
Language and Culture courses and of the English language courses offered at the
Accademia.

She published the Arte e Metodo (Guerra Edizioni) series. She is the
author of Comunicare in italiano (Hoepli), In italiano. I corso 1, 2, 3 (Hoepli)
and .it, the first Italian language course for smart devices (goWare). In 1999, she
wrote a experimental thesis that was part of the research for the first Italian
draft of the Portfolio Europeo delle Lingue. This research project was promoted by
the Council of Europe and took place in Umbria from 1996-1999.

From 2000 2005, she
translated into Italian texts of the American poet Murray Bodo, O.F.M. Icarus in
Assisi (Minerva Edizioni and the works of Albert Camus: L’artiste en prison, (Davar
I/2003), René Char (Davar II/2005), Metafisica cristiana e neoplatonismo (Diabasis,
2004), and La devozione alla croce (Diabasis, 2005).

In 2004, she taught Italia
language and culture courses at the University of California at Santa Clara. From
2005-2009, she taught seminars in Applied Linguistics in Malta, Hungary, Poland,
Greece, Sweden, Argentina, Uruguay, Croatia, Slovenia, Germany Belgium, China,
Spain, and Norway. Since 2017, she has been a PhD student at the University of
Portsmouth (UK where she conducts research in second language acquisition.
