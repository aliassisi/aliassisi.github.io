Arte e metodo is a series of texts dedicated to the teaching and learning of Italian. Each text focuses on
one specific aspect of the language in the context of a more general linguistic competence. The title of
this series reflects the authors' opinion that teaching and learning a language is a science, whilst
writing and speaking it is often an art.
