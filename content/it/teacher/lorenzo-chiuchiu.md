## About

Lorenzo graduated with a degree in Aesthetics from the University of Florence
under the supervision of Anna Giannatiempo Quinzio and Sergio Givone.

He received a
PhD in Philosophy and Sciences of the Text from the University of Siena. He
published essays on Hrabal, Hoffmann, Camus, Char, Baudelaire, Hölderlin, Nietzsche,
Bloch, De Angelis, and Quinzio. He is a member of the editing committee of the
review Davar. He edited into Italian Métaphysique chrétienne et Néoplatonisme
(Gallimard) and La dévotion à la Croix (Gallimard) by Albert Camus. He translated
into Italian and edited Mallarmé, la lucidité et sa face d’ombre by Jean-Paul
Sartre (Gallimard).

He published two poetry books: Iride incendio (La Vita Felice)
and Sorteggio (Marietti 1820).
