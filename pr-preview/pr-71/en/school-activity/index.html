Activities, facilities and workshops

Learn Italian while discovering Italian culture, lifestyle, and traditions.

Activites and Workshops

Learn Italian and discover Italian culture, lifestyle and traditions through activities and workshops included in all our group courses:

    • 1 weekly class outside the classroom: conversations, guided tours in Assisi and contemporary Italy
    • 1 weekly pronunciation class
    • 1 biweekly workshop (bookbinding, illuminated manuscripts, Italian craftsmanship, pasta making, Italian lifestyle)
    • free entrance in 4 museums in Assisi
