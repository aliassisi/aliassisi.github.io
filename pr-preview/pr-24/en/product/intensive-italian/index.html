Intensive

The morning belongs to the group: classmates from all over the world, learning together. The afternoon belongs to you: a one-to-one lesson, shaped around your goals.

Course structure

Morning group lessons Afternoon one-on-one lessons
Monday 4 lessons Free
Tuesday 4 lessons 2 lessons
Wendsday 4 lessons Free
Thursday 4 lessons 2 lessons
Friday 4 lessons Free

Duration: min. 1 week, 22 lessons per week
Levels: intermediate and advanced
Lessons per day: 6 twice a week, 4 three times a week
Morning session: 4 lessons a day, from Monday to Friday
Afternoon session: 2 lessons a day, twice a week
Class size: morning session min. 5 – max. 15. One-on-one in the afternoon
Books: course books, ebooks and access to interactive self-study activities on Moodle 

Details

The course combines the advantages of a group work and individual work. The afternoon lessons are set aside for deepening the student’s language proficiency with emphasis on conversation and fluency.  It is particularly suitable for beginners who wish to learn quickly as well as for students at the intermediate or advanced level whose skills are often uneven. In fact this course offers the chance to work on any points of weakness. In the one-on-one lessons, intermediate and advanced students can choose the topics they prefer and work on the specific vocabulary needed for their academic or professional purposes. The placement test and interview will be online, prior to the start of the course.
